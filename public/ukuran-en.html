Size Guide

Find the right size for every WearScrubs product. All measurements use centimeters (cm).

How to Measure Correctly

A

Chest Circumference

Measure around the widest part of the chest, just below the armpits. The tape should be flat, not too tight or loose.

B

Waist Circumference

Measure around the natural waist (the narrowest part of the torso), just above the navel.

C

Hip Circumference

Stand upright with feet together. Measure at the widest part of the hips and buttocks.

D

Top Length

Measure from the shoulder/neck junction, straight down to the desired bottom hem.

E

Inseam

From the crotch to the ankle. Use your favorite pair of pants as a reference.

F

Head Circumference (Cap)

Measure around the head just above the ears and forehead, where the cap will sit.

Tips: If your measurements fall between two sizes, we recommend choosing the larger size for optimal freedom of movement during long shifts. For tops, use the chest measurement as the primary reference.

How to Measure Head Circumference:

Gunakan pita ukur yang fleksibel. Letakkan pita melingkari kepala tepat 1–2 cm di atas alis dan di atas telinga, lalu pertemukan ujung pita di bagian belakang kepala.

Wearing Tips:

Tuck all hair inside the cap before tying the strings. For long hair, gather it with a hair tie first before putting on the scrub cap.
