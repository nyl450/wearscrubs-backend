To ensure the right size, follow these measurement steps:

  • Chest — Measure under the arms, around the widest part of the chest.
  • Waist — Measure around the natural waist, just above the navel.
  • Hips — Stand with heels together, measure the widest part of the hips.
  • Inseam — From the crotch seam to the bottom of a well-fitting pant.
  • Top Length — From the shoulder/neck junction to the bottom hem of the top.
